Marble was also live-tested in the real game. **All four are Jackdaw bugs; the Rust engines were right.** Fixed in `src/sim_patches.py` (commits eacdcbe, 8e2aa48), with tests in `tests/test_sim_backend.py`. Real-replay parity is unchanged at 329/336, with every number the same.

1. **Madness + Ceremonial Dagger: Jackdaw wrong.** `card.lua:2870`: the whole `setting_blind` branch requires `not self.getting_sliced`. Madness marks its victim at once (`2890-2891`), and so does Dagger (`2954-2956`), in board order. Jackdaw collected every result first and rolled Madness in the apply step. Fix: `setting_blind_slices_in_order` fires jokers one at a time, skips sliced ones, and rolls `pseudoseed('madness')` on Madness's turn (only when there is a victim).
2. **Mr. Bones: Jackdaw wrong.** `state_events.lua:1015-1021` (`G.FUNCS.evaluate_round`): a saved round adds `{dollars = 0, name='blind1', saved = true}`. Fix: `saved_round_pays_no_blind_reward` pays no blind reward when chips < blind chips.
3. **Satellite: Jackdaw wrong.** `card.lua:2012` pays $1 per distinct key in `G.GAME.consumeable_usage` with `set == 'Planet'`. `set_consumeable_usage` fills that on every use, pack picks included. Jackdaw's handler read `ability.planet_types_used`, which nothing ever sets, so it always paid $0. Fix: `satellite_counts_used_planet_types`.
4. **Marble Joker: Jackdaw wrong (live-tested).** Reading the code, the Stone card's emplace and `draw_card` events are queued during `setting_blind`, ahead of new_round's `G.deck:shuffle('nr'..ante)`. I tested it live: `scripts/diagnostics/marble_live_test.py` drives the real game (BalatroBot `add` / `set`) and the sim in lockstep, then compares the full 53-card order after the big-blind select. On EPN12S4E, KMTUIA73 and 725LK1MF the Stone card was at positions 14, 28 and 7. The real order matched the "shuffled in" rule card for card on all 3 seeds, and Jackdaw's "bottom, after the shuffle" rule on none. Evidence: `docs/samples/marble-live-test.json`. Fix: `marble_stone_joins_before_shuffle`.
